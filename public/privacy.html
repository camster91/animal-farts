💨 PootBox Privacy

No account needed

We don't ask for your name, email, or any account. Just open the app and start playing.

What is stored

Built-in sounds, pages, and local recordings are stored in browser storage on this device. When the app is online, a recorded sound is also uploaded to the PootBox server so it can survive a reload and support sharing. The server stores the audio, its display name and emoji, a random device identifier, and basic timestamps.

Microphone

The microphone is only used while you are actively recording a sound. We never listen in the background. Local recordings remain playable offline. Online uploads happen only after a recording is made and can be removed from the app; shared recordings may be accessible to anyone who has the share code.

Sharing and deletion

Sharing creates a temporary code for a recorded sound. Share codes expire after 30 days. Deleting a recording from the app requests deletion of its server copy, while local browser storage may retain the offline copy until it is removed from the device or browser data is cleared.

No ads, no analytics

We don't track you, we don't show ads, and we don't sell your data. PootBox is made for kids and families.
